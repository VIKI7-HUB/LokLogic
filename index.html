ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

A clearer harvest decision.

Harvest Saathi explores Tech4Bharat problem statement 6.1: farmers selling at the wrong time, place and price. It compares simple net-proceeds scenarios using the values entered on this page.

What this demo does not doIt does not fetch live prices, forecast prices, verify local costs, or make a sale. Prefilled figures are illustrative; an imported CSV is not independently verified. Confirm real offers and costs locally.

The future-price range and spoilage estimate are user-entered assumptions. They are shown as scenarios, not predictions.
